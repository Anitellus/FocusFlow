Focus Flow Complete Program Guide

1. Strict 3-Tier Hierarchy (Main → Sub → Component)

Organize work into exactly three structural depths: Main Project (Tier 1), Sub-Project (Tier 2), and Component Project (Tier 3). Tasks exist strictly at the lowest leaf level of any branch; if a project contains children, tasks cannot be added directly to the container.

2. Time-Blindness Sweep & The Amber Flow Zone

The central timer features an analog radial progress ring that fills gradually toward your target estimate. Exceeding your target activates the warm Amber Overtime Halo ("Flow Zone"), reframing extra duration as exploratory focus rather than punitive failure.

3. Park & Resume Context Anchors

Pausing the clock prompts a lightweight anchor: "Where did I stop?" and "What is my immediate next 60s action?". When returning, a prominent launchpad banner sits above the timer with one-click resumption to eliminate reactivation paralysis.

4. 1-Click Micro-Step Decomposer

Break overwhelming tasks into atomic ≤5-minute chunks directly on the focus stage. Complete micro-steps to earn dopamine chimes and burst momentum.

5. Non-Punitive Rolling Momentum Battery

The Analytics dashboard tracks sustained effort using a 7-day rolling battery that fills up when completing 5 out of 7 active days. Missing a day gently drains energy rather than wiping all progress down to zero.
