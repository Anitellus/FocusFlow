Focus Flow Complete Program Guide

1. Strict 3-Tier Hierarchy (Main → Sub → Component)

Organize work across three structural depths: Main Project, Sub-Project, and Component Project. Tasks exist strictly at the lowest leaf level of any branch. If a project has children, tasks cannot be added directly to the container.

2. Time-Blindness Sweep & The Amber Flow Zone

The central timer features an analog radial progress ring that fills gradually toward your target estimate. Exceeding your target activates the warm Amber Overtime Halo ("Flow Zone"), reframing extra duration as exploratory focus rather than punitive failure.

3. Park & Resume Context Anchors

Pausing the clock prompts a lightweight anchor: "Where did I stop?" and "What is my immediate next 60s action?". When returning, a launchpad banner sits above the timer with one-click resumption to eliminate reactivation paralysis.

4. 1-Click Micro-Step Decomposer

Break overwhelming tasks into atomic ≤5-minute chunks directly on the focus stage. Complete micro-steps to earn dopamine chimes and burst momentum.

5. Calendar & Temporal Horizons

The calendar view separates work into digestible cognitive horizons (Today, Next 7 Days, and Gentle Recalibration). Dates that have passed are never displayed as punitive failures; they can be moved forward with a single click (+1 Day, +3 Days, +1 Week).
